# Suspended native coroutines: what they cost today

Generated by `tclsh9.0 audit/storage-execution-owner/tools/stacks.tcl` (STORAGE-EXECUTION-OWNER-CENSUS-1.md). K request coroutines are opened and all suspended at once (each keeps one String, `head`, across its suspension), then resumed to completion. The native backend (cranelift); heap counts are the managed heap's (native::allocationReport, scratch storage on); the resident set is a standalone executable's peak (the whole process).

Pages first touched per suspended coroutine, the slope of the minor faults between K = 1024 and K = 4096: 1.08 (4 KiB pages on this host: about 4.3 KiB each).
Address space reserved per live coroutine stack: 8 MiB + 64 KiB guard (runtime/coroutine.rs, MAP_NORESERVE on Linux), so K = 4096 reserves about 33024 MiB of address space.
What each one keeps across its suspension is one String (`head`: "GET /items/" and at most 4 digits, a native block of at most 40 bytes) and its loop state.
